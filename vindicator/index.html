Devlog

What landed 2026-07-16 → 2026-07-25, by area.

Art & Worldbuilding

07-17 → 07-25
  • Nine races, one visual language each — hulls, stations, and cities settled in a full taste pass.
  • First complete ship-and-station catalog rendered for every race; the Kithar picked their hull from a four-way sheet — the scarab won.
  • Homeworlds now read from orbit: per-race terrain shaders with coast-following pinpoint city lights.
  • Black hole rebuilt with gravitational lensing and doppler shading; the fold reads as suction instead of a whirlpool.

Combat

07-19 → 07-22
  • Heavy Railgun readability: the tracer wake now drags behind the round; an Overcharged variant joins the family.
  • Energy weapons share one capacitor per weapon family, and a weapon can be authored to wind up before it fires.
  • A closing doctrine can boost into reach of a retreating gun.

Economy & Stations

07-19 → 07-25
  • Mining stations you own: place one on a belt, it fills a hold with ore — haul it to market and watch the economy move.
  • Stations burn Power Cells to stay lit, and can be emptied but never destroyed.
  • Danger maps to richness in every belt; remote market data sits behind subscription tiers; haulers keep their cargo through gates.

Fleet & Command

07-19 → 07-25
  • Crew & officers: a slot ladder and three officer types with real effects, run from the dock's FLEET screen — recruits fly hauler and trader routes themselves.
  • Shift+click and Tab select flights; dock and hail collapse into one interact verb; F9 opens a perfect-scanner inspector.

Universe & Travel

07-21 → 07-25
  • Every race got an authored home system — starting with Ennead, the Trine ocean home — and the territory-holders now field their own factions.
  • Six new planet types, survey designations for unnamed bodies, and The Verge: a frontier system built around the mining loop.

Interface & Sound

07-16 → 07-22
  • Map selection panel and pause menu rebuilt as proper scenes; codex articles now unlock through discovery.
  • Weapon SFX rebuilt from sourced samples, plus two new retro tracker tracks.
